## Objective
To analyze IBM HR employee data and identify key factors driving employee attrition - with actionable recommendations to reduce turnover and improve employee retention.

## Tools Used
 - **Python** (Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn) - Data cleaning, EDA, visualization
 - **SQL** (SQLite) - Business queries and agrregations
 - **Power BI** - Interactive dashboard
 - **Git & GitHub** - Version control

## Dataset 
- **Source:** Kaggle - IBM HR Analytics
- 1,470 employees
- 35 columns covering demographics, job details, satisfaction scores
- **Target variable:** Attrition (Yes/No)
- **Overall attrition rate:** *Will be shown after the analysis!*

## Business Questions
1. What is the overall attrition rate and which department has highest attrition?
2. Does salary affect attrition?
3. Does overtime cause higher attrition?
4. Which age group has highest attrition?
5. Do job satisfaction and work-life balance affect attrition
